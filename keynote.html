Invited Keynote

Saturday, October 3, 2026 · 11:00–11:40 · Room: TBD

Mario Romero Senior Associate Professor, Linköping University

As we may think next: immersive sense making from the memex to the chatbot

Abstract

For thousands of years, humans have advanced civilization by externalizing thought through language, writing, diagrams, books, and digital technologies. These cognitive artifacts allowed knowledge to persist beyond individuals and accumulate across generations. Today, AI introduces a new possibility. Rather than merely creating more external representations, it allows people to engage with knowledge, ideas, and collaborators, both human and machine, as part of an increasingly continuous cognitive process of unprecedented scale.

Drawing on examples from immersive analytics, visualization, and human-centered AI, this keynote argues that the next frontier is not artificial intelligence but immersive cognition: the re-internalization of knowledge, collaborators, and cognitive artifacts into a continuous process of thinking.

For thousands of years, humanity advanced by externalizing thought. Perhaps the next step is to internalize it at scale.

Bio

Mario Romero is a Senior Associate Professor at Linköping University and National Technical Manager of InfraVis, Sweden’s national research infrastructure for data analysis and visualization. His group's research explores the convergence of AI, immersive visualization, XR, digital twins, and conversational interfaces. Through collaborations with industry and the public sector, they develop human-centered technologies that transform complex data into adaptive understanding and learning experiences.
