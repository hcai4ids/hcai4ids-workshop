Call for Participation: HCAI4IDS @ NordiCHI 2026

We invite research students, academics, and industry practitioners to submit extended abstracts to the NordiCHI 2026 workshop on Human-Centered AI Support for Immersive Data Sensemaking.

We welcome work on human-AI collaboration, immersive analytics, XR, visualization, multimodal interaction, adaptive AI collaborators, trust calibration, and evaluation approaches for AI-supported immersive data sensemaking.

Important dates:
- Extended submission deadline: September 3, 2026, 23:59 AoE
- Notification of acceptance: September 9, 2026
- Camera-ready deadline: September 19, 2026
- Workshop date: October 3, 2026

Submission format:
- 2-4 page extended abstract, excluding references
- ACM double-column proceedings format
- PDF submission by email
- Submissions do not need to be anonymized

Submit to: organizers.hcai4ids@gmail.com
Workshop website: https://hcai4ids.github.io/hcai4ids-workshop/
